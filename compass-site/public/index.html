Built for HN Group

Designed from the ground up around HN Group identity, security and speed.

Workspace

Floating vertical tabs

Keep pages full-width while your tabs float in a sleek collapsible rail — compact icon dock or expanded list, with drag ordering, pinning and keyboard shortcuts.

Identity

HN Account & PIN lock

Sign in once with HN Group Auth. Compass remembers only a revocable OS-encrypted credential and opens straight onto your six-digit PIN lock.

Network

Built-in HN VPN

Connect and disconnect the full-tunnel HN WireGuard VPN straight from the toolbar, check live handshake status and share the tunnel with HN Secure Access Terminal.

Operations

Built-in VPS SSH terminal

Open tabbed SSH sessions directly to the HN Group server over the VPN with host-key pinning, themed terminal colours and zero saved passwords.

Navigation

HN workspace & smart bar

Type any HN company or service name in the address bar — even with typos — and jump straight there, with an official HN Group badge on verified sites.

Personalisation

Five themes & private library

Switch seamlessly between Midnight Lavender, Daylight, Deep Ocean, Forest and Rose Quartz. History, bookmarks and downloads stay strictly on your device.

Security & updates

Verified installers, device approval and silent in-place updates.

Compass checks device approval on devices.hngroup.org.uk, isolates every website in a Chromium sandbox, and verifies SHA-256 hashes before installing updates in place.

Sandboxed pagesWebsites run isolated from browser controls, tokens and SSH.
OS-encrypted storageKeychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows, Secret Service on Linux.
Automatic updatesVerified in-place updates restore your open tabs when Compass reopens.
