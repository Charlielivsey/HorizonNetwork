# HN Group HR

https://hr.hngroup.org.uk — PM2 `hn-hr`, loopback 5680, `/opt/hn-hr`. Install with `sudo bash hr-site/install.sh <certificate-email>`.

Dedicated HN Auth client `hr`, independent host-only staff session. The site uses the established HR screens, approved Midnight Lavender theme and dropdowns, with a restricted proxy to the private staff service at 3250. The public Staff Portal has no HR navigation tab, blocks migrated HR APIs with HTTP 410 and redirects old page routes here with their query strings. Existing leave data remains in `/var/lib/hn-staff-portal/leave.json`, written by one backend only. No separate accounts or leave data are copied.

All existing annual leave, sickness, other absence, TOIL, work patterns, return-to-work forms, team calendar, shutdown days and management settings remain available. Default annual allowance is 74 days. Additional days require the staff member’s request, reason and current direct supervisor’s decision. Pending plus approved extra requests are capped at 10 days per person per calendar year; approved extras apply only to that year, subject to the existing carry-over rules. No self-approval or HR-only substitute approval for supervisor requests. Where no direct supervisor is assigned, an explicit staff request automatically tops up approved additional days to 10 for the selected year; repeated requests cannot grant more. Pending requests for that year are replaced and retained as cancelled history.

HR overview (`/` or `/home`) links to Leave & Absence (`/leave`), Rotas & Timesheets (`/rotas`, including `/api/timesheets`), Probation & reviews (`/reviews`), Goals (`/objectives`), 360 feedback (`/feedback`), Flexible working (`/flexible`), Exit interviews (`/exit-interviews`, plus the staff response at `/exit-interview`), Staff documents (`/staff-files`), Training (`/training`) and HR cases (`/cases`). Navigation and page search respect existing permissions. Finance stays at https://finance.hngroup.org.uk/ with an external link. Staff Portal search, notifications and legacy links resolve to the appropriate HR destination. HR sign-in preserves approved tool destinations and queries.

All existing management, supervisor, ownership, shared-document and case-access checks remain in the original handlers. All HR records and uploaded files stay in `/var/lib/hn-staff-portal`; the private backend remains the sole writer. Docs and Contracts files remain live read-only links, including per-person Docs grants, contract ownership checks, source failure messages and the 20-second retry. The proxy exposes only the listed HR API families, shared session/assets/avatars and notifications; write requests require the HR origin. Nginx allows the existing 20 MB staff/case uploads and a 40-second response timeout for linked sources.

Checks: `node --test hr-site/test.cjs hr-site/integration.test.cjs staff-portal/leave-extra.test.cjs staff-portal/staff-documents.test.cjs`. Add `HN_HR_BROWSER=1` to exercise every migrated tool for manager and ordinary staff, search, refresh, mobile layout and the absence of the Staff Portal HR tab. Browser fixtures use isolated temporary data and Auth/backend processes, never production records. `CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE` may specify an existing Chromium executable.

Employee Management (`/employees`), Jobs (`/jobs`), Joiners & leavers (`/onboarding`) and Job descriptions (`/job-descriptions`, including position/reporting settings) also live on HR. Its People menu and overview respect the original staff, recruitment, onboarding and job-description permissions; ordinary staff keep jobs/applications/referrals and read-only job descriptions. Personal and supervisor-assigned joiner/leaver tasks now appear in full on the HR overview, with the original per-task access checks. Their Staff Portal navigation, onboarding home widget, recruitment reminders and team hiring section have been removed. Roles & permissions and unrelated People tools remain on Staff Portal. Old page URLs redirect with queries; migrated private APIs (including positions) return 410 on the public portal.

The existing HN Account one-time handoff works from HR after adding a staff record and from employee cards, preserving the account-create destination. Account creation remains on HN Account. The Discord-link prompt for staff managers follows Employee Management onto HR. `/api/public/jobs`, public applications and application-reference checks remain available through the existing public Careers integration and use the same recruitment records. Integration fixtures cover staff record creation/editing/permanence, actual HN Account ticket exchange, positions/descriptions, staff applications/referrals, public applications, hiring and assigned checklist tasks, including permission denials and persistence after a backend restart.

## HR workspace layout

HR has a persistent navigation sidebar and compact command bar matching the redesigned Staff Portal. Its grouped overview, attention links and complete personal checklist panel use the existing permission-filtered HR tools and data. The sidebar name/photo opens account actions in a keyboard-accessible popover; account controls stay collapsed by default. Compact page headings keep icons beside their titles, and retained HR forms, tables and dialogs use the same HN component treatment. The deprecation notice and portal-only calendar/chart scripts are removed from HR HTML.

`public/assets/hr-ui.js` and `hr-ui.css` are HR-owned and served by its proxy, so changes do not alter the Staff Portal or Finance layout. The CSS adapts approved portal workspace components under an HR-only root selector. Four optional `HNHR` hooks in the shared frontend provide existing identity, filtered tools, route changes and modal lifecycle; no new backend/data writer or authentication flow is introduced. Deploy both HR assets, `server.js` and the shared frontend hooks together. The HR installer copies its public assets.

Desktop QA uses `HN_HR_BROWSER=1` (with `CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE` if needed) and checks manager/staff views, the collapsed account menu, keyboard focus, every HR route, icon/title spacing, dialogs, search, assigned tasks, refresh, permission denials and original data workflows. Mobile QA is currently skipped at the user's request; `HN_HR_MOBILE=1` is an explicit opt-in for later use.

## Section navigation and sidebar disclosure

HR and Staff Portal share `staff-portal/public/assets/workspace-navigation.js` and `.css`. HR loads exactly one copy of each through the existing restricted asset proxy, alongside its own `hr-ui` adapter. The HR sidebar contains Overview, Notifications, and four sections: Your work, Development, People & recruitment and HN Group services. Section overviews use `/home?section=work|development|people|services`; existing page URLs remain unchanged. The shared component renders permission-filtered overview cards and top-of-content child navigation on section overviews and child pages, including active section/page state and browser history. External service links remain in Services.

The desktop header toggle hides/reopens the entire sidebar, updates its accessible state, frees the content width and remembers the choice separately for HR. Collapsing or navigating closes floating account controls. The account button toggles its native popover open/closed through the common animation controller; outside-click, Escape, focus restoration and reduced motion are retained. HR keeps its dialog first-control focus and Tab containment. Mobile QA remains paused; current checks are desktop only. Deploy the common assets and routing hooks with the HR adapter before publishing its versioned HTML.
