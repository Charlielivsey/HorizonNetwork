About HN Group

Connected businesses, one way of working.

HN Group brings together Horizon Network, Horizon Media Group and HN Group Management. Within Horizon Network, Horizon Advertising and Horizon Development bring advertising and software expertise alongside digital infrastructure. The businesses share people, systems and standards across the group.

Infrastructure

Digital services and hosting from Horizon Network, monitored around the clock.

Advertising

Display advertising and campaign management from Horizon Advertising, with clear reporting.

Software

Custom software and web applications from Horizon Development, from first idea to launch.

Media

Content and media from Horizon Media Group, the group’s media arm.

How we work

Shared people, shared systems, shared standards.

Being one group means our companies work as one team. A client of one Horizon business can draw on the skills of all of them.

  1. One team across the group. People work across companies, so the right expertise is always close by.
  2. Built to keep running. Services are monitored 24/7, and the systems behind them are built and run in-house.
  3. Clear and straightforward. Plain answers, honest reporting and no surprises.
