Four specialist companies, one way of working.

How we work

Shared people, shared systems, shared standards.

Being one group means our companies work as one team. A client of one Horizon business can draw on the skills of all of them.

  1. One team across the group. People work across companies, so the right expertise is always close by.
  2. Built to keep running. Services are monitored 24/7, and the systems behind them are built and run in-house.
  3. Clear and straightforward. Plain answers, honest reporting and no surprises.
