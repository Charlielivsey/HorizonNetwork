Before you appeal

Help us understand your appeal.

A clear explanation and the relevant details make your appeal easier to review.

01

Identify the decision

Tell us which server or service is involved, what action was taken and when it happened. Include any reference you were given.

02

Explain your reasons

Let us know why you are appealing and what you would like the team to reconsider. Include relevant context or evidence.

03

Include your details

For a Discord appeal, include your username and user ID if you know it, so the team can identify the account involved.

Contact appeals

Ready to get in touch?

Send your appeal to appeals@hngroup.org.uk. If you have already emailed, you can reply to that conversation with any further details.

Only include information relevant to your appeal. Never send passwords, PINs or sign-in codes.
