Article 1 — Executive directives

1.1 — This Operations Policy applies to Horizon Network (HN). References to HN Group (HNG) describe the oversight and review functions expressly set out in this policy; they do not extend Horizon Network’s executive-directive powers to HN Group or other companies.

1.2 — Only the substantive Chairman of the Board of Directors (Board Chairman) or Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Horizon Network may issue an executive directive (ED). A person acting as, or temporarily covering, either position does not acquire this power.

1.3 — An executive directive may override any policy, rule or decision within Horizon Network while the directive is in force. This is an internal authority and must be exercised subject to applicable law and Horizon Network’s governing documents. Directives remain subject to the review and repeal routes in Article 2.

1.4 — Every directive must be recorded in writing and have a unique reference, a descriptive title, an issue date, the issuer’s full name and substantive position, and a clear statement of its purpose.

1.5 — The Authority field must state “Article 1 of the Horizon Network Operations Policy”. Where a directive exercises a specific power in another Article, it must also identify that supporting provision and comply with its scope and requirements.

1.6 — A directive must identify the persons, functions or activities affected; the decision or instructions; those responsible for implementation; and any deadlines. It must state when it takes effect and whether it continues until withdrawn, expires on a stated date or is subject to review.

1.7 — Any interim arrangements and the treatment of decisions or activities already under way must be explained where relevant. Policies and responsibilities outside the directive’s stated scope continue to apply.

1.8 — The issuer must communicate the directive to the Horizon Network Board of Directors, the HN Group Chief Executive, affected Horizon Network leadership and those responsible for implementation. Persons within its scope must comply while it is in force.

1.9 — A directive assuming control of Horizon Network or dismissing one of its directors must identify Article 4 as a supporting provision. A directive suspending policy sections must identify Article 5. The issuing authority remains Article 1. A specific power reserved to the Board Chairman is not conferred on another office-holder by this Article.

1.10 — A directive may be amended, extended, replaced or withdrawn in accordance with the issuing authority in this Article and the repeal routes in Article 2. Each subsequent instruction must identify the original directive, the change and its effective date, and must be communicated to affected recipients. Earlier versions must be retained.

Article 2 — Review and repeal of executive directives

2.1 — An executive directive may be repealed through any of the following four routes:

2.1(a) — Office-holder repeal: either the substantive Board Chairman or the substantive CEO of Horizon Network repeals the directive.

2.1(b) — Horizon Network Board vote: the full Board of Directors votes on repealing the directive. If the vote carries, the Board Chairman must issue a new directive repealing the original.

2.1(c) — HN Group executive intervention: the HN Group Chief Operating Officer (COO) or CEO considers that the directive is not in the best interests of Horizon Network or the wider HN Group. The HN Group COO issues a statement retracting the directive. This intervention may be appealed to the HN Group Board.

2.1(d) — HN Group Board intervention: the HN Group Board decides that the directive is not in the best interests of HN Group or Horizon Network and orders the HN Group COO to issue a statement retracting it.

2.2 — A repealing directive or retraction statement must identify the original directive, the authority relied upon and the date and time the repeal or retraction takes effect. The decision and any instructions for implementing it must be communicated to the affected leadership and staff.

2.3 — A Board decision, executive intervention, appeal and appeal outcome must be retained with the original directive. Any consequential changes to responsibilities, interim instructions or suspended policy sections must also be recorded and communicated.

Article 3 — Dissolution and replacement of the Board

3.1 — The CEO of Horizon Network may issue a special resolution dissolving the Horizon Network Board of Directors at any time.

3.2 — A new Board must be selected within 72 hours of the dissolution taking effect.

3.3 — The special resolution must be recorded in writing, identify the Board concerned and state when the dissolution takes effect. The record must show the resulting 72-hour deadline and when the replacement Board was selected.

3.4 — This Article concerns the Horizon Network Board only. It does not confer a power to dissolve the HN Group Board or the board of another company.

Article 4 — Assumption of control of Horizon Network

4.1 — The substantive Board Chairman of Horizon Network may assume full control of Horizon Network by issuing an executive directive under Article 1. This power does not apply to other companies within HN Group.

4.2 — The directive must identify Horizon Network and state that the Board Chairman is assuming control under this Article. It must specify when it takes effect and communicate the immediate instructions to Horizon Network’s leadership.

4.3 — While the directive is in force, the Board Chairman exercises full control of Horizon Network. Its leadership must follow the Chairman’s directions, and existing delegations must be read in accordance with those directions.

4.4 — Under this Article, the Board Chairman may dismiss a director of Horizon Network without notice, subject to the requirements in Article 1.3. The dismissal must identify the person and directorship concerned and state when it takes effect. No notice period is required under this internal policy.

4.5 — The directive and any dismissal must be recorded and communicated to the affected leadership and the HN Group Chief Executive. Relevant staff records, responsibilities and system access must be reviewed to reflect the decision.

4.6 — The Board Chairman must communicate any subsequent change to the directive, including the return of control to Horizon Network’s leadership. The record must show when control was assumed, changed or returned.

Article 5 — Suspension of Horizon Network policy sections

5.1 — The substantive Board Chairman of Horizon Network may issue an executive directive under Article 1 suspending specified sections of a Horizon Network policy. This power does not extend to HN Group policies or policies of other companies.

5.2 — The directive must identify each policy, its version or date where available, and the exact sections to be suspended. It must state the reason for suspension and the persons, functions or activities to which it applies.

5.3 — The directive must specify when the suspension takes effect and its end date or review arrangements. Any interim instructions replacing the suspended sections must be stated clearly. Sections outside its scope continue to apply.

5.4 — The Board Chairman must communicate the directive to the Horizon Network Board, the HN Group Chief Executive, affected Horizon Network leadership and those responsible for implementing the policies. Relevant staff must be informed of the suspension and interim instructions.

5.5 — The directive must be retained with the affected policy records. The record must show the sections suspended, the effective period and any extension, variation, withdrawal or reinstatement.

5.6 — Suspension does not itself permanently amend or repeal a policy. The Board Chairman may vary or withdraw the directive and must communicate when the suspended sections resume operation, including following a repeal or retraction under Article 2.

Article 6 — Document ownership and governance records

6.1 — The Horizon Network Board of Directors is the document owner for this Operations Policy and must ensure that executive directives and their amendments, repeals and retractions are retained in an identifiable governance record.

6.2 — Records must retain the directive reference, issuer, issue date, issuing authority, supporting provisions, scope, instructions and effective period, together with subsequent decisions, notices and implementation records.

6.3 — Recording or implementing a directive does not confer authority to issue directives on any other office-holder. The restriction on acting office-holders in Article 1.2 continues to apply.

6.4 — Permanent changes to this policy must be recorded in a revised policy version. A directive that suspends a provision must not be presented as a permanent amendment merely because the suspension has taken effect.
