# HN Appeals

Approved-family service wordmark with an original review document/return icon, outlined Plus Jakarta Sans lettering and HN brand steps. SVG masters and transparent PNG exports are provided in dark text, white text and an app icon.

`render.cjs` requires `fontkit` (or an ESM entry point supplied in `HN_FONTKIT`) and uses the repository's local Plus Jakarta Sans font. PNGs are rendered from the matching SVGs at 1400 pixels wide (wordmarks) and 512 × 512 (icon). Publish matching files to Brand centre together.
