Privacy notice

How we use your information

This notice explains what personal information HN Group Limited collects through this website, why, and what your rights are.

Who we are

HN Group Limited (“HN Group”, “we”) is the data controller for information collected on horizon-network.co.uk. HN Group brings together Horizon Network and Horizon Media Group. Horizon Advertising and Horizon Development are part of Horizon Network. If your message is about one of those companies, we may share it with them so they can reply.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and anything else you choose to give us (phone number, organisation and your message), plus the IP address it was sent from to help us block spam.
  • Job applications: your name, email address, optional phone number, your CV and anything you tell us about why you’re applying.

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking, and doesn’t load anything from other companies’ servers.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, provide our services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To consider your job application. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into an employment contract, and our legitimate interest in recruiting.

We never sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

Who can see it

Enquiries are seen only by the people in the group who handle them. Applications are seen only by our recruitment team and the hiring manager for the role. Everything is stored on our own server, not with third-party form or recruitment services.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries are deleted automatically a year after we’ve finished dealing with them.
  • Unsuccessful job applications (including CVs) are deleted automatically 6 months after the decision. If you’re offered and accept a job, your application becomes part of your staff record.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it or ask us to restrict it. To do this, send us a message using the contact form, choosing “Something else”. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
